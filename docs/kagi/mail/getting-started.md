# Kagi Mail

## General Information

### What is Kagi Mail?

Kagi Mail is a premium email service developed by Kagi.
It aims to provide a high-quality, feature-rich email experience that integrates with the Kagi ecosystem.

The product philosophy focuses on craftsmanship, speed, user agency, and open standards, avoiding the "race to the bottom" found in free, ad-supported services.

### What is the pricing model?
Pricing has not been finalized.
*   **Philosophy:** Kagi is not competing with "free" or ad-supported models.

## Getting Started

Kagi Mail can be accessed via https://kagimail.com. 
Simply use your Kagi login details to sign in.

When signing in for the first time, you are prompted to select your email username (the part before the @ in your email address).
Usernames must be at least 5 characters long.
Shorter usernames may be made available later.

On the same screen, you can set a display name.
Having one makes it less likely that your message will be flagged as spam by the recipient's email service.
